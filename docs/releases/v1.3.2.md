# SnakeyOS 1.3.2

- **Screen resolution fix.** Choosing a resolution the display cannot show (more than its video memory holds, or a width that is not a multiple of 8, like 1366) no longer leaves a black screen with a frozen pointer.
  - The video driver refuses such a mode before touching the screen, and goes back to the mode that worked if the adapter refuses one.
  - The desktop falls back to the largest mode that works (1280×800, 1024×768...), also at login, and a saved resolution that does not work is replaced.
  - Settings only lists the resolutions this screen can show; 1366×768 became 1360×768.
